Mastering robotics (okay... maybe not mastering, but at least being able to build something simple) has always been my dream.

I came across this [amazing influencer, Steven Gong](https://www.youtube.com/@stevengongg), and he blew my mind with the many projects he has completed. What impressed me most, though, was his resilience: he had to overcome so many obstacles across all of his projects! That's a trait I'd like to develop too, so I decided to start my own journey into robotics.

I started with ROS 2 and I'm tracking my progress on [GitHub](https://github.com/wwang107/ros2-learning). So far there's just a kickoff "hello world" (a publisher and a listener). My end goal is to build a robot that picks up the dirty laundry from my floor XD. Let's see how it goes!
